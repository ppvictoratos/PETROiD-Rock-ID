# Privacy Policy for PETROiD

**Last updated:** October 2026

PETROiD does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

- **Camera / Photo Library.** Used only to capture a photo of a rock so the app can
  identify it. Photos are processed and stored entirely on your device via SwiftData —
  they are never uploaded anywhere.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.** PETROiD makes zero network
  requests.
- **No accounts.** There is nothing to sign in to and nothing tied to your identity.
- **Data deletion.** Deleting the app deletes all locally stored rocks and photos.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the App Store support URL
listed on PETROiD's App Store page.
